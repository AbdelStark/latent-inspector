What is Self-Supervised Learning?

The labeling bottleneck

Supervised learning

Needs human labels for every image

ImageNet: 14M images, years of work, millions of dollars

Self-supervised learning

No labels needed

The internet: billions of images, zero annotation cost

The trick: invent a task that requires no labels but forces the model to understand the image's structure.

Different questions, different understanding

Self-distillation: "Here are two different views of the same image. Produce the same representation for both."

Latent prediction: "I masked part of the image. Predict what the representation of the masked part should be."

Video prediction: "Here's a video. Predict the representation of the next frame."

Multi-teacher distillation: "Match the outputs of all these specialist teachers simultaneously."

Each question creates a different learning pressure.
That pressure sculpts the geometry of the representation.

The Four Models

DINOv2 — The Segmenter

ArchitectureParametersEmbeddingMethod
ViT-L/14304M1024-dimSelf-distillation

A student network matches a slowly-evolving teacher across different augmented views of the same image. The model learns that objects are the things that remain stable when everything else changes.

Result: emergent object segmentation — without ever seeing a segmentation label.

Meta FAIR — Oquab et al., 2023

I-JEPA — The Predictor

ArchitectureParametersEmbeddingMethod
ViT-H/14632M1280-dimLatent prediction

Masks large regions of the image and predicts the representation of the missing patches — not the pixels. Predicts meaning, not appearance.

Result: the most spatially differentiated representation. Every patch encodes unique context.

Meta FAIR — Assran et al., 2023  |  Based on Yann LeCun's JEPA architecture

V-JEPA 2 — The Temporal Thinker

ArchitectureParametersEmbeddingMethod
ViT-L/16304M1024-dimVideo prediction

Predicts future video frames in latent space. Even on a static photo, it carries an implicit prior about motion and time.

Result: the richest dimensional spread. It sees the elephant and understands it could walk.

Meta FAIR — Bardes et al., 2025

EUPE — The Generalist

ArchitectureParametersEmbeddingMethod
ViT-B/1686M768-dimMulti-teacher distillation

Distilled from multiple specialist teachers: DINOv2, depth estimators, segmenters. One compact model that's decent at everything simultaneously.

Result: extreme compression with radical geometric consequences.

Meta FAIR — Zhu et al., 2026

Same backbone. Same image. Same patches.

The only difference is the training objective.

What is a Representation?

From pixels to vectors

Image (224 x 224 px)

  ↓  split into 14x14 pixel patches

256 patches

  ↓  each patch projected to 1024 dimensions

256 vectors of 1024 numbers

  ↓  Transformer: each patch attends to every other

256 refined vectors = the representation

262,144 numbers. That's what we analyze.

PCA — Principal Component Analysis

The representation lives in 1024 dimensions. PCA finds the directions of maximum variation — the axes that matter most.

Map the top 3 directions to Red, Green, Blue → a color image.

Same-colored regions = the model considers those patches similar.

Colors are relative to each model. You can't compare "red" across models.

How Each Model Sees

→ switch to terminal: run compare with --format html

DINOv2 — Emergent Segmentation

Sharp boundaries. Elephant body = one color. Background = another.

Self-distillation forces consistency across augmented views.
The most consistent thing across crops, rotations, and color shifts... is the object.

→ point to the PCA image: "Notice how the elephant is cleanly separated from the background"

I-JEPA — Fine-Grained Spatial Detail

More colors. Trunk, legs, ears, background each distinct.

The prediction objective forces each patch to be unique.
If adjacent patches had identical representations, the model couldn't predict which one is missing.

V-JEPA 2 — Maximum Diversity

The most colorful image. The most directions in representation space.

Trained on video, it encodes spatial structure plus temporal priors.
More factors to encode = more dimensions used = more colors in the PCA.

EUPE — Smooth Universality

Smooth gradients. No sharp boundaries. No segmentation.

Optimizing for every task simultaneously produces a representation
so compressed that the spatial structure DINOv2 develops... isn't there.

Isotropy

Do patch embeddings point in diverse directions...
or do they all cluster in a narrow cone?

Picture a room of 256 compasses.
High isotropy = every compass points a different direction.
Low isotropy = they all point north.

What happened?

Multi-teacher distillation collapsed the directional diversity into one compact universal feature direction.

The differences between patches are tiny variations around that one direction. EUPE compressed its entire vocabulary into 17 inflections of a single word.

Top-10 variance: EUPE packs 88.8% of its information in just 10 components.
V-JEPA 2 needs 38 components to reach 90%.

Cross-Model Similarity

CKA — Centered Kernel Alignment

Do two models organize their representations in similar geometric structures?

Like two restaurant critics: do they agree on which restaurants are similar to each other?

1.0 = identical geometry    0.0 = completely unrelated

EUPE ↔ I-JEPA: zero. Not low. Zero.

Three findings

1. DINOv2 ↔ V-JEPA 2 = 0.358 (highest). Same ViT-L architecture → shared geometry. Architecture constrains more than expected.

2. I-JEPA ↔ V-JEPA 2 = 0.275. Same JEPA framework, but image vs video → different geometries. Modality matters more than paradigm.

3. EUPE ↔ anything ≈ 0. Multi-teacher distillation created something geometrically alien to every single-objective model.

The Distillation Paradox

EUPE was trained on DINOv2. DINOv2 was its teacher.

CKA between EUPE and DINOv2: 0.044

The student absorbed the knowledge but completely reorganized the filing system.

What Shapes Perception?

The hierarchy of forces

1. Training objective — the dominant force
Self-distillation, latent prediction, video prediction, multi-teacher distillation → four fundamentally different geometries

2. Architecture — the container
Same ViT-L = more similar than same paradigm in different ViTs

3. Modality — image vs video
Same JEPA framework, different modalities → different geometries

4. Model size — the compression budget
86M params forces extreme compression that 304M never needs

Different World Models.
